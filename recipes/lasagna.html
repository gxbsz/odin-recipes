Bolognese Lasagna

Welcome to Bolognese Lasagna recipe.

See the ingredients below, follow the instructions and enjoy this awesome lasagna!

Prep time: 1 hour and 15 minutes

Serving: 6

Ingredients

  • 500g lasagna sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 2 packets of tomato sauce
  • 300g sliced ham
  • 300g mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Oregano to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion until translucent.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Pour in the tomato sauce and season with salt, black pepper and oregano.
  5. Let the sauce simmer over low heat for approximately 15 minutes.
  6. Spread a layer of sauce across the bottom of a baking dish.
  7. Add alternating layers of lasagna sheets, sauce, ham and mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with sauce and cheese.
  9. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake in a preheated oven at 350°F for approximately 30 minutes.
  10. Remove the foil and bake for another 10 minutes, until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  11. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving.
